# De-card vocabulary — the review contract

The shipped list/detail surfaces (settings, programs, exercises) speak one
visual language. **Check every conversion and every new surface against this
page.** It restates DESIGN.md § De-card vocabulary with the live components.

## Hairlines, not shells

Content sits on the page background, framed by muted hairline dividers — not
inside rounded card shells. Prefer the primitives in `src/components/ui/` over
re-typing the recipes.

<Canvas of={SectionStories.StackedZones} />

### The exact recipes

| Piece | Recipe | Primitive |
|---|---|---|
| Hairline | `border-b border-b-border/60` | — |
| Caps header | `font-display text-base uppercase leading-none tracking-wide text-muted-foreground` | `Section` |
| Divider list | `divide-y divide-border/60 border-b border-b-border/60` | `DividerList` |
| Divider row | `flex items-center justify-between gap-4 py-4 transition-colors outline-none hover:bg-muted/50 focus-visible:bg-muted/50` | `DividerRow` |
| Empty words | `px-1 py-6 text-center text-sm text-muted-foreground` | `EmptyWords` |

A chart or hero sits **above** a hairline rather than inside a shell.

### The dashed variant

Dashed hairlines are the quarantined / pending voice — "present, but not
product". Reach for it deliberately, not for visual variety.

<Canvas of={DividerListStories.Dashed} />

## An empty state is a sentence

Not a boxed apology, not an illustration. Say what is not there and — when
there is one — what to do about it.

<Canvas of={EmptyWordsStories.WithNextStep} />

## One volt

**One volt moment per screen**: the primary action, or the single live element.

On revisit surfaces — lists you scan repeatedly — per-item volt stacks and is
**banned**. A zone heading or a single delta carries the accent; declines and
ordinary values render quiet. The stat grid below has exactly one volt delta,
and that is the rule, not the example.

<Canvas of={StatTileStories.Grid} />

## Chips are controls, words are labels

Pill/chip styling means "you can press me" — toggle chips, segmented controls,
the rest pill. Metadata is words in the muted ink. **Never decorate a label as
a chip.**

## Keep-list (do not de-card)

Card shells stay where the shell *is* the meaning:

- **Sheets, dialogs, popovers** — elevation is the point of an overlay.
- **Coach chat** — message bubbles are the conversational idiom.
- **StatTile** — a tile grid is the scannable record wall.
- **Article / content-preview cards** — external previews read as clippings.
- **Actor chips** — provenance markers are chips by design.
- **Control clusters** — rest pill, segmented controls, toggle chips.
- **Media tiles** — photos and share cards need a bounded frame.
- **Form fields** (`Input`, `Textarea`) — fields need enclosure.
- **`src/components/ui/card.tsx`** — the primitive stays for keep-list use.
- **Nav-drawer hover shapes** — the drawer's own interaction vocabulary.
- **Programs-landing active hero** — explicitly kept as shipped.

## The lint ratchet

`eslint.config.mjs` bans `rounded-2xl` and `bg-card` (via
`better-tailwindcss/no-restricted-classes`) outside two allowlists:

- **`CARD_SHELL_KEEP`** — the keep-list above. Permanent.
- **`CARD_SHELL_RATCHET`** — files still carrying pre-conversion shells,
  grandfathered so the ban could land.

**Every conversion PR must remove its files from the ratchet.** It only ever
shrinks, never grows, and new surfaces never join it — they use the primitives
from day one.
