The plan · Week 1
Day 1 · Squat
3 exercises · ~60 min

3×10 · moderate, add 2.5 kg on completion

3×15+ · last set AMRAP
Day 2 · Overhead Press
OHP · Deadlift · Dumbbell Row · ~55 min
Day 3 · Bench
Bench · Squat · Cable Face Pull · ~60 min
Day 4 · Deadlift
Deadlift · OHP · Lat Pulldown · ~55 min
Weeks 2–5 repeat these days — T1 lifts add 5 kg when you hit all reps, T2 add 2.5 kg, T3 chase the AMRAP. Week 4 backs everything off.
Who it’s for
You’ve run a beginner LP and stalled. GZCLP keeps the linear engine but adds a second and third tier so volume doesn’t vanish when the top sets get heavy. Miss a day — the plan waits; nothing resets.
