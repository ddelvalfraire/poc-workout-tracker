# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy explains how [COMPANY] ("we", "us") handles **consumer health
data** in [APP NAME], as required by the Washington My Health My Data Act
and similar laws in Nevada and Connecticut. It applies in addition to our
[Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md); for consumer health data, this policy
controls.

## Categories of consumer health data we collect

- Workout and exercise data: sessions, exercises, sets, repetitions, loads,
  perceived-effort ratings, and training programs.
- Body measurements: bodyweight and other measurements you record.
- Progress photos you choose to add.
- Notes you attach to workouts or exercises (which may describe physical
  condition, e.g., soreness or injury).
- Messages you exchange with the AI coach, which may contain health
  information you include.
- Derived training metrics computed from the above (e.g., estimated training
  maxes, volume totals, readiness suggestions).

We collect this data **only from you** — from what you enter into the app.
We do not collect consumer health data from other sources, and we do not use
inferences to derive health data from non-health data.

## Why we collect and use it

We collect and use consumer health data **solely to provide the services you
ask for**: recording your training, displaying your history and progress,
deriving your program's targets, and generating coaching responses. We also
use it as necessary to secure the Service and comply with law.

We do **not** use consumer health data for advertising or marketing, and we
do **not** use it to train AI models.

## How we get your consent

- **Collection:** at signup, we ask for your consent to collect the health
  data the Service needs to function.
- **Sharing with our processors:** we separately ask for your consent before
  any consumer health data is shared with the service providers below.
- You may withdraw consent at any time in Settings or by contacting
  [CONTACT EMAIL]. Because this data *is* the Service, withdrawing consent
  for collection means closing your account; we will delete your data as
  described below.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell consumer health data, and we will not share it with third
parties for their own purposes. It is processed only by service providers
acting under contract on our behalf:

| Processor | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase (database hosting) | Storing your training and body data |
| Vercel (application hosting) | Serving the application |
| [LLM PROVIDER] | Generating AI coach responses from your messages and training context |
| Sentry (error monitoring) | Diagnostics if an error involves in-flight data |

Our analytics provider (PostHog) is deliberately **not** a processor of
consumer health data: analytics events carry only counts, durations, and
coarse categories — never exercise names, loads, bodyweight, photos, or note
text.

We will disclose consumer health data if legally compelled (for example, by
a valid court order), and only to the extent required.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** the consumer health data we hold about you (your data is also
  visible and exportable in the app at any time);
- **Withdraw consent** to our collection or sharing of your consumer health
  data;
- **Delete** your consumer health data. Deletion requests are honored for
  the live database promptly and for backups on their ordinary aging
  schedule; we also instruct our processors to delete.

To exercise any right, use the in-app Settings controls or email
[CONTACT EMAIL]. We will respond within the time required by law. We will
not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If we deny a
request, you may appeal by replying to our response; if your appeal is
denied, you may contact the Washington Attorney General (or your state's
attorney general).
