# Slice 1: mainstream barbell strength programs

Classified against `arena2/synthesis` (algebra.ts, structure.ts, programs.ts, rationale.md). Construct names are the ones those files use. "Telescope" means a later step's target reading `performed` or `prescribed` of earlier steps. "Peer" means `peerState` / `c.peer` reads of another slot's state. Three facts shape most rows:

- A slot's plan does not know which day it is on. A lift that trains differently on Mon/Wed/Fri becomes one slot per day, and the non-owning slots peer-read the owner.
- A step's count is a plan position. It may never read performed sets. `stepUntil`'s stop predicate sees only the step's own last set plus earlier steps, it is post-tested, and its target is fixed for every repeat.
- `pos` exposes `week`, `trainWeek`, `role` and `slotSession`, but the language has no `mod`. A per-week pattern is a `schedule` (possibly of booleans or of whole sessions).

25 programs. Primary buckets: NATIVE 20, ONE-NODE 5, MODEL CHANGE 0, OUT OF SCOPE 0. StrongLifts is NATIVE for its core, and its layoff rule is ONE-NODE. Westside is borderline MODEL CHANGE (see its row). Fragments that belong outside the engine are noted inline.

## Patterns this slice demands (deduped)

1. **Step-local accumulators for repeat steps.** `stepUntil`'s self handle needs `repsSoFar` (Σ reps over this step's logged sets) and `setsDone`. This drives J&T 2.0 MRS, Building the Monolith's "100 reps", and Greyskull's "15 total reps" variant (the event-side twin: `EventQuery { q: 'repsTotal', steps }`).
2. **A pre-tested, self-referential repeat step** (`stepWhile(go, max, target(self))`). Zero iterations allowed; the target may read the step's own previous set. Drives 5/3/1 Jokers and any prescribed "work up in X% jumps" ramp. Patterns 1 and 2 can be one former if `stepUntil` gains a pre-test mode and its target becomes a function of `self`.
3. **Athlete-chosen set counts** (`count: { k: 'range', min, max }`). Drives Candito's optional sets, and also the "8–12 sets" DE prescriptions and "1–3 fatigue sets" in RPE programs that are not rule-driven.
4. **Accommodating resistance** as a set field and performed field (`accom: q[mass]` at top). Drives Westside DE. It also forces a decision on whether `ev.load`, `ev.e1rm` and tonnage include band/chain tension (it ties to the app's logging-type weight semantics).
5. **Elapsed-time read** (`input('daysSinceLast')` or `EventQuery 'daysSince'`). Drives the StrongLifts/SS layoff resets. `pos` is week-indexed, not gap-aware.
6. **Author-declared enums.** Stage machines (GZCLP T1/T2, Texas stall rotation, Cube day type, StrongLifts 5×5→3×5→1×5) want enums, but `Enums` is a closed engine registry. Numeric counters plus `nth`/`schedule` work around it at a prose cost. Moving enum declarations into definitions removes the engine edit per program.
7. **Sub-cycle boundaries inside a repeating calendar.** 5/3/1 Forever leaders/anchors needs "end of each 3-week cycle" inside a longer calendar. Today that is a `weekEnd` handler gated by a boolean `schedule`, since `cycleEnd` fires only per full calendar pass and there is no `mod`. A `segmentEnd` event, or calendars as lists of named segments, would make it declarative. Not blocking.
8. **Per-day variation of one lift is always slots plus peer reads.** Madcow, Texas, SS light day, nSuns, Smolov and Sheiko all model "the same lift, different day" as separate slots with one owner (single-writer) and peer reads, and cross-day judgement uses peer reads inside `weekEnd` handlers. This works everywhere in the slice. A `pos.day` read is a possible ergonomic addition, not a requirement.
9. **Large fixed tables** (Sheiko, Smolov, Rippler, Juggernaut) are expressible as `schedule` rows but costly to author. This argues for a compact tabular import, or a `table(week × day → session)` sugar that elaborates to schedules. Ergonomics, not expressiveness.
10. **Already-covered load-bearing constructs, confirmed by this slice:** the telescope (APRE, Bulgarian back-offs, RTS drops, meet attempts), effort-only top sets with self-selected load (RTS, Bulgarian, Westside ME, J&T RM), `bands` tables for AMRAP→increment (nSuns, APRE, Greyskull), computed increments (Juggernaut), `peakOn` fixed-phase macros with typed handoff (Smolov, meet peak, Candito), and role-bound `Use` transformers for taper/deload.
